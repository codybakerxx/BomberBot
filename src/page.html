How to add the BomberBot app!

  • Full screen
  • Plays offline

    Room invites

    Get a ping when a friend opens an online room and wants you in.

    To receive notifications, be sure to:

    1. Add the BomberBot app to your Home Screen (the steps above) and open it from there.
    2. Switch on Get room invites below, and tap Allow when your phone asks.

    Then here is how it works:

    1. A friend makes an online room and taps the bell at the top of their lobby.
    2. You get Cody wants to play · Room BKTW is open. Tap it to jump straight into their room.

    Hosting? Tap the bell yourself to call everyone in. Room invites are the only notifications BomberBot sends, a room can ring once a minute, and you can turn them off here any time.
