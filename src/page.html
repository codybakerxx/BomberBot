Pick a colour with the arrows, a dot or a swipe across your robot, then Ready (or double-tap the dot). If a CPU wears it, the CPU takes your old colour. Picking another colour takes Ready away until you press it again. Online, each player will do the same.

Players

Tap a CPU player to change its colour or level. The bin removes it at once (Undo brings it back). Up to 8 players: you and 7 CPU players. With more than 6, the arena grows by two tiles each way. CPU players are always ready.

This browser is not saving settings, so they last only until you close the game.

Keyboard

Arrow keys or W A S D to move. Space, X, J or Enter drops a bomb. Shift, Z or K lifts a bomb and throws it. Escape opens the menu and goes back.

In the menus: Tab moves between buttons, ← and → change a setting, Q and E switch pages.

Game controllers are not supported yet.
